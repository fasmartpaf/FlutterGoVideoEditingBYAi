# Constrained Edit Proposal V1

Provider id: `CURRENT_OPENSCREEN_EDIT_PROPOSAL_V1`

## Role

Turn Edit Plan (+ optional Closure) into **precise, executable-shaped proposals** that prove:

1. Evidence justification  
2. Landing (SOURCE_MEDIA_TIME)  
3. What must survive  
4. What could be damaged  

## Non-goals

- Does not execute tools  
- Does not mutate AxcutDocument  
- Does not apply trims/zooms/crops  
- Does not export  

`proposedCall` is always `proposal_only` / `notExecuted: true`.
